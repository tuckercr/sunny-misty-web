Meet the forecasters

Two pets, one sky. Sun, cloud, rain, storms, snow, heat, cold and night each get their own scene, with a fresh caption every day.

Sunny

A golden retriever who loves a walk, as long as it won't mean soggy or scorched paws. Sunny checks the next 24 hours and picks the best time to head out: right now, later today or tomorrow morning.

Misty

A grey-and-white tuxedo cat who watches the weather from the window. Misty keeps an eye on the clouds, the rain and the moon, and occasionally knocks the Wi-Fi off the shelf.

Everything you need, nothing you don't

A simple, friendly weather app for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Best walk time

Sunny weighs rain, temperature, daylight and air quality, so you're not caught in a downpour or out at the hottest hour.

Paw check

Sun-baked pavement can be far hotter than the air. Paw check estimates how hot it is underfoot, even on a mild day, and warns you about icy paths in winter.

Air quality

Smoky or polluted day? Walk times steer clear of the worst hours, and on the worst days Sunny settles for potty breaks.

Hourly and 7-day

Plan the next few hours or the whole week, with the chance of rain and hour-by-hour detail for every day.

Rain radar

See showers moving in on a live radar map of your area.

Gentle reminders

A morning briefing at 8:30 and a nudge at the best walk time. Both are optional.

All your places

Add as many cities as you like, or use your current location, each in its own time zone.

Works offline

Your last forecast is saved on your phone, so it's there even without a connection.

Private by design

No account, no ads and no tracking. Your cities stay on your device.

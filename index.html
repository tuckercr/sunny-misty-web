Meet the forecasters

Two pets, one sky. Their mood changes with the weather, so you can tell what it's like outside at a glance.

Sunny

A golden dog who loves a walk, as long as the weather won't mean soggy paws. Sunny looks ahead at the hourly forecast and tells you the best time to head out, today or tomorrow.

Misty

A grey cat who watches the weather from the window. Misty keeps an eye on the clouds, the rain and the moon, and occasionally knocks the Wi-Fi off the shelf.

How the walk card decides

Last updated: November 14, 2026. The iPhone and Android apps work the same way.

The walk card shows for people who said they have a dog. It answers one question: when is the best time to walk today? The app checks the hour under way first and then each hour after it, and the first step below that matches decides what the card says.

  1. Hurricane force wind?

    Hurricane force wind (Beaufort 12) now, gusts included, or forecast in the next 6 hours. This card shows for everyone, dog or not.

    Now
    Danger: "Hurricane force winds". "Stay inside, away from the windows."
    Coming
    Danger: "Hurricane force winds from 6 PM". "Get everyone inside before then."

  2. Is now a good time?

    The hour under way passes every check in What makes an hour good.

    Headline
    "Now is a great time"
    Caption
    The first of these that applies:
    • The good spell ends within 6 hours: "Good until 5 PM, then rain." (or then maybe rain, snow, storms, very windy, too hot, hot pavement, too cold, bad air). Another spell later today adds "Good again 6 to 8 PM."
    • Air that's one band short of bad: "Air's not great. Short walks for older and flat-faced dogs."
    • Sunset within 3 hours: "Gets dark in about 2 hours.", "Gets dark in about an hour." or "Getting dark soon."
    • Otherwise no caption.

  3. A good hour later today?

    Before dawn the rest of the night counts as today, so a good hour at 7 AM is still "today".

    Headline
    The spell of good hours, up to the first hour that isn't good: "5 to 8 PM", "11 AM to 1 PM". "Around 8 PM" when the forecast ends first.
    Caption
    • Another spell later today comes first: "Or 7 to 9 PM." The title becomes Best Walk Times.
    • Then why not now: the "Bad now" caption from the captions when there is one, otherwise the "Better later" one ("The rain's gone by then."). Nothing when it's only too dark now.

  4. Nothing good left today

    What it says depends on the time of day, in the city's own time zone.

    Morning
    "Not a good day for walkies"
    Afternoon
    "No more walkies today"
    Caption
    The "Bad now" caption when there is one, otherwise the "Nothing today" one ("Rainy out.").
    Night, with a good hour tomorrow
    The only time the card looks to tomorrow: "Tomorrow 7 to 10 AM", and maybe "Or 2 to 4 PM."
    Night, with none tomorrow either
    "No walkies tomorrow either"

    Night is after sunset, by the clock. Morning is before noon.

Storms on their way. Whatever the step, likely thunderstorms in the next 6 hours add "Storms from 4 PM." to the caption, unless it already says storms are coming.

An hour is good only when it passes every check. These are the default limits; the app's remote settings can change them. When several checks fail, the worst one gives the reason.

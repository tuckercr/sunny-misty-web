Common questions

How do I try the test version?

On Android, open the test page on Google Play, tap Become a tester, then install Sunny & Misty from Google Play. On iPhone and iPad, install Apple's TestFlight app, then open the TestFlight link and tap Accept. Test versions update on their own, and you can send feedback from TestFlight or by email.

Why doesn't the app show the weather where I am?

Sunny & Misty needs location access to show your current location. Open the app's Settings and tap Location to allow it, or add your city by name instead.

When do notifications arrive?

There are two, and both are optional: a morning briefing at 8:30 and a nudge at the best time for a walk. To choose which you get, open the app's Settings and tap Notifications. The walk nudge needs "I have a dog" switched on.

How is the best walk time chosen?

Sunny looks through the next 24 hours for a daylight hour that's dry and comfortable, with clean enough air and pavement that's safe for paws: right now, later today or tomorrow morning. If you don't have a dog, switch off "I have a dog" in Settings to hide it.

What is Paw check?

Sun-baked pavement can be far hotter than the air. Paw check estimates how hot it is underfoot and says when it's getting hot, too hot or dangerously hot for paws, or icy in winter. It's an estimate, so if in doubt, hold the back of your hand on the pavement for seven seconds: too hot for your hand is too hot for paws.

Why does Sunny say "Potty breaks only"?

The air is unhealthy right now, from smoke, pollution or ozone, so keep trips outside short. Walk times skip hours with bad air, so if it clears later, the card says when.

Why does the app say it's offline?

Misty knocked the Wi-Fi off the shelf. The app couldn't reach the weather service, so it shows the last forecast it saved. Check your connection and refresh.

How do I switch between Celsius and Fahrenheit?

Open Settings and choose your units, or leave it on System default to follow your phone's settings.

Where does the weather come from?

Current conditions come from OpenWeather, forecasts and air quality from Open-Meteo and the radar from RainViewer.

What data does the app collect?

Very little, and nothing that identifies you. See the privacy policy for the details.

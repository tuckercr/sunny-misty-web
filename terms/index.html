You know your dog and your surroundings best: always check conditions yourself and use your own judgement.

By using the app you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

The weather and walk advice is a guide

The app shows forecasts from third-party weather services and turns them into advice: the best time for a walk, the Paw Check pavement temperature, air quality, and warnings about heat, cold, wind and storms. This advice is an estimate:

  • Forecasts can be wrong, out of date or missing, especially for a particular street or a particular hour.
  • Pavement temperatures are estimated from the weather, not measured. Real surfaces vary with sun, shade, colour and material.
  • Every dog is different. Age, breed, size, coat and health all change what weather is safe, and the app doesn't know your dog.
  • Notifications are planned ahead on your device and may arrive late, or not at all, depending on your phone's settings and battery saving.

Before a walk, check the conditions yourself: feel the pavement with the back of your hand, watch the sky, and keep an eye on your dog. If in doubt, ask your vet.

Severe weather

Sunny & Misty is not an emergency or severe weather warning service, and a warning may not appear in time or at all. In severe weather, such as storms, hurricanes, extreme heat or flooding, follow the warnings and advice of your local weather service and emergency authorities.
